# Acme Company Information

## Deliveries
- Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days.
- Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days.
- Delivery times begin after an order has been confirmed.

## Returns
- Customers can return unused items within 30 days of delivery.
- To start a return, contact support@acme.example with the order number.

## Support
- Customer support is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
- Contact support@acme.example for help with an order.
